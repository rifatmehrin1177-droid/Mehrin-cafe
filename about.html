The Journey & Vision of Mehréa Café

Mehréa Café was born from a deep passion for combining modern Pinterest-inspired purple aesthetics with Bangladesh's vibrant youth café culture. Founded by visionary food entrepreneur Rifat Tabassum Merhin, the name "Mehréa" embodies warmth, elegance, and comforting artistic vibes.

Merhin noticed a gap in Dhaka's café scene: people were looking for more than just coffee. They wanted a sanctuary with soothing purple ambient lighting, cozy study spots, acoustic lo-fi tracks, and a menu filled with handcrafted delights—from authentic Japanese Matcha & refreshing Mojitos to creamy thick shakes and traditional homemade caramel puddings.

Every single item on our 30+ dish menu is personally tested and curated by Merhin using premium local and imported ingredients to ensure unmatched flavor, texture, and visual perfection.

"Mehréa is created as a sweet home away from home—where every cup, bite, and purple spotlight tells a story of comfort." — Rifat Tabassum Merhin
